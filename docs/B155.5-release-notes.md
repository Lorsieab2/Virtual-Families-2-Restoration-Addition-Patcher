# B155.5 - Full-game calibrated older-villager mortality

B155.5 changes only the optional Older Villager Mortality curve and the local
build plumbing needed to produce the release from this self-contained checkout.
All other gameplay remains based on B155.

## Mortality rule

The patch retains the stock birthday check, stock old-age death path, and stock
nutrition threshold:

- `T = 55 + clamp(active food groups, 0, 4)`
- `n = max(0, displayed age - T)`
- `intensity = 0.00365*n + 0.06*max(0, n - 55)`
- `annual hazard = min(0.999999, 1 - exp(-intensity))`

The probability is rounded half-up to millionths. The helper makes exactly one
native `GetRandom(1000000)` roll on each eligible 20-tick birthday. The late
term begins after effective age 110. The first quantized cap is effective age
318, and the cap always retains one non-death result, so there is no hard
maximum age and no birthday has certain old-age death.

Disabling the option restores the untouched stock block: ten percentage points
of annual old-age mortality for every effective year after the same threshold,
reaching certainty ten effective years later.

## Full-game calibration

Calibration uses the requested full-game population of 60 adults: 30 maximum
generations times two adults capable of reaching old age per generation.
"Reaches age A" means the adult survived every old-age roll through age A-1;
the roll performed at age A is not counted against reaching that birthday.

The constant-group cases are calibration boundaries. Actual adults may gain or
lose active food groups during life; each birthday uses the groups active at
that check, exactly as the native path supplies them.

The committed validator writes the detailed ages 55, 60, 65, 70, 75, 80, 85,
90, 95, 100, 105, 110, 115, 120, and 122 table to the ignored `outputs` folder.
It reports individual reach odds, full-game reach odds, exact-age deaths, and
expected games per success for all five nutrition cases.

## Preserved behavior and exclusions

- The optional hook still replaces only the annual old-age decision.
- `SetHealth(0, OldAge)` remains the native successful-death path.
- Online and time-away catch-up retain the existing annual physiology timing.
- Sickness, starvation, exhaustion, pregnancy, furniture, events, goals,
  achievements, cheats, and all other causes and systems are unchanged.
- Organic-food state does not affect this curve.
- The planned age-122 longevity achievement is not implemented by this build;
  B155.5 makes reaching that age possible but does not claim a new trigger.

## Validation contract

The B155.5 validator requires:

- a nondecreasing annual hazard at every tested effective age;
- no 100% old-age hazard and no hard maximum age;
- the exact one-year shift for each of 0-4 active food groups;
- modal and median ages shown above;
- the full-game age-110 and age-122 probability bands shown above;
- deterministic simulations of at least one million adults for every constant
  food-group case, agreeing with the analytical model within five standard
  deviations.

Live gameplay QA remains distinct from structural, analytical, and simulated
validation and is not claimed until performed in the game.

Automated results: all 178 unit tests pass with one intentional skip. All 16
linked executable layouts have unique hashes and pass Holiday-positive/
negative validation, exact embedded mortality-table and helper checks, three
nonoverlapping runtime-flag checks, idempotent enable, and exact disable
restoration.
